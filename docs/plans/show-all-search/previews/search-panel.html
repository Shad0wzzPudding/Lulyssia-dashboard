"Show all" window: the search's chips

When the search has options (a comma for "any of", a "!" to hide), a panel appears behind the cards and the chips sit in a tab at its corner (top-right in A, the owner's choice). A plain search, with no comma or "!", shows no panel, the same way the page's search bar shows no chips. Pick a look, then type in the window or tap an example.
