Items 1 and 2

Wrapped lines line up under their text

When a long line wraps, the wrapped part starts under the line's text instead of the left edge: after its spaces, and after its bullet ("- ", "• ", "1. "), like Word. Only the look changes; the saved text is the same.

Where: Tasks, Events and Interests cards, and Home (Today lists, calendar, details window, In progress, Upcoming)

The same while you type

The description editor (task, event and interest forms) wraps the same way, and updates straight away as you type, Tab, Space or add a bullet. Everything else in the editor works exactly as now.

The search bar works like a filter

report, slides finds items with either word. !draft hides items with "draft". lab, !draft: has "lab", not "draft". Words without a comma stay one phrase as now, and quotes keep a comma or ! as text. Chips under the bar show how a filter was read; the empty bar shows an example.

After the build
