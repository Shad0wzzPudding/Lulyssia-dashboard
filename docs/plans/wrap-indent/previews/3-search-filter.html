Search as a filter

A working search bar over sample tasks. Type words separated by commas, and put ! before a word to hide items that have it. The chips under the box show how your search was read. Intent only: the build uses the app's real search bar.

A comma means:
Try:
  • A: report, slides shows items with "report" or "slides". B: only items with both.
  • !draft hides every item with "draft"; with other words, lab, !draft means: has "lab", and not "draft".
  • Words without a comma stay together as now: report slides looks for that exact phrase.
  • To look for a comma or a ! itself, put it in quotes: "!important".
  • Title and description are searched, ignoring capitals, as now.
