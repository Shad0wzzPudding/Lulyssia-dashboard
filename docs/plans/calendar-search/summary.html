  • Same search as the pages: title and description, commas mean "or", "!" hides; chips after "Results for" for a search with options.
  • Finds what the calendar shows: unfinished tasks and all events. One row per item with both its dates.
  • Upcoming first, soonest first; then "Past", most recent first. Only dates with a match stay highlighted.
  • Picking a row jumps the calendar to its start date (or due/end date); "← Results" goes back. ✕ or Esc ends the search.
