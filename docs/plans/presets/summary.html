  • A stacked carousel that loops: drag or swipe, ‹ ›, ← →, or tap a card behind. The most used preset is in front. Built with the motion package.
  • Cartoon cards in black, white and light blue: a white glass slab for the name with a band in the tag's colour under it, light-blue comic dots fading up, a white butterfly in the corner. They slide down from above; a slanted label per tag above each (past 16 letters: the first two and "…").
  • Tap the front card to pick it, then "Apply preset": an empty title takes the name; the description gets the format (asking "Add below / Replace" if it has text); tags are added; Deadline/End = start + X (start = now if empty).
  • Hold a card or press ⓘ for its details, with Pick, Edit and Delete. "+" makes a new preset. Sounds: expand, edit, success, cancel.
