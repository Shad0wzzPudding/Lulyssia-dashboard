Items 1, 5 and 6
1

Same-day items show one time range

A task or event that starts and ends on the same day shows "09:00 – 13:00" instead of a start and an end side by side, with a Start and end today badge beside its type. The half-green, half-red stripe on the calendar's list stays. On another calendar day the badge reads "Start and end same day".

Where: calendar's day list and details window, Today's Tasks, Today's Events

Edit tasks and events right from Home

A pencil after each task's and event's name opens its edit form on top of Home. Update or Cancel and you're still on Home, with the change shown. The form is the same one the Tasks and Events pages use, so everything in it works the same.

Where: Today's Tasks and Events, In progress, Upcoming, the calendar's day list and details window

Description editor & Settings

Items 2, 3, 4 and 8
2

Tab indents lines

Tab adds 6 spaces to the start of the caret's line, or of every selected line. Shift+Tab takes up to 6 back off. The lines stay selected, so you can press it again. To leave the box with the keyboard: Esc, then Tab.

Where: task, event and interest forms · Today Tab jumps to the next field

3

Space adds a space to selected lines

With whole lines (or 2+ lines) selected, Space adds one space to the start of each line instead of deleting them. With only part of a line selected, Space works as usual. This is "Quick space", the default.

8

Editor setting: Quick space or Normal

A new white Editor card at the end of Settings chooses what Space does with lines selected. Quick space (item 3) is the default; Normal makes Space replace the selection as in other editors. Saved to your account, so it follows you to every device. Tab and the Tab button aren't affected.

LINE

Item 7
7

LINE keeps the indents

Descriptions in LINE messages (morning letter, day-before notice, starting-soon card, missed-deadline nudges) keep each line's leading spaces, so what you indent in the app is indented on LINE too. The 5-line and 300-character limits stay.

Where: every LINE message with a description
