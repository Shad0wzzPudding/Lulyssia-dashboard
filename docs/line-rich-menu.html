Lulyssia LINE Menu Pages

Option C with your changes: two pages switched by the tabs on top. "Choice" puts the four everyday commands in the drawer's jagged speech choices, pointing at Lulyssia in her slanted portrait frame. "Settings" holds the eight on and off switches in the style of today's menu. Both are drawn at LINE's real menu size.

  • Tabs switch pages inside the menu (LINE's own page switching), so they send nothing into the chat.
  • Choice opens first. The highlighted choice is decoration, like the cursor in the drawer; all four can be tapped.
  • Settings adds the four that were missing: Notice on and off, and the morning letter's start and stop.
